Your teal.fm listening history, visualized.

Sign in with your Atmosphere (ATProto) handle or upload a Spotify listening history ZIP to generate a fast, GitHub-style activity heatmap.

Uses standard ATProto OAuth (PKCE + DPoP). Read-only access to your public PDS records.

or

Upload your Spotify Extended Streaming History (.zip). Music tracks played ≥ 30s generate the exact same heatmap as teal.fm.

Direct from your PDS

Reads fm.teal.feed.play and legacy fm.teal.alpha.feed.play records directly from your ATProto repository.

Built for 3G speed

Server-rendered inline SVG, zero external fonts or JS frameworks, gzipped responses under 12 KB, and PostgreSQL caching.
